About the Academy

A Sanctuary for the Arts, Rooted in Heritage

Founded 15 years ago, Gazal Darbar is a music and dance academy in Thazhe Chelari, Malappuram, Kerala, where traditional Indian arts meet modern creativity. We offer training in Hindustani Music, Carnatic Music, Mappilapattu, Classical Dance, Cinematic Dance, Tabla, Guitar, Keyboard, Harmonium, Flute, Violin, Triple Drums and Drawing. Our academy is dedicated to helping students learn with discipline, personal attention and a passion for the arts.
