About the Academy

A Sanctuary for the Arts, Rooted in Heritage

Founded 15 years ago, Gazal Darbar was built on a simple belief that classical discipline and modern creativity are not opposites, but partners. Based in Chelari, Malappuram, our academy brings together the ragas of Hindustani and Carnatic tradition, the melody of Harmonium with the rhythm of Tabla, and the grace of classical dances like Bharatnatyam, mohiniyattam, alongside the contemporary language of guitar, keyboard, cinematic dance and drawing.

Mission

To nurture every student's artistic voice through disciplined training, individual attention and a genuine love for the craft.

Vision

To become the region's most trusted address for classical and contemporary arts education, honoured for both technique and soul.

Values

Discipline, authenticity, warmth and the belief that every student deserves a stage.
